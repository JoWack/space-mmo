# Borlash City — A-07, round (task 167)

`Borlash.fbx` and `Borlash_manifest.json` are generated by `tools/greybox/a07_borlash_city.py`. Never
edit either by hand: the numbers live in the script, and rerunning it is the only way these change.

The working `.blend` and the preview renders live outside the repository, in
`D:/Documents/SpaceMMOAssets/Blender/Stations/BorlashCity/`. Task 154's square blockout is in
`superseded_task154/` there and in git at commit 509b9c1.

## Regenerate

From the repository, in PowerShell:

```powershell
& 'G:/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Blender/blender.exe' --factory-startup --background --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/greybox/a07_borlash_city.py
```

Append `-- --check-only` to measure without writing anything, or `-- --render` to write the seven
preview renders as well. Inside a running Blender (the MCP session), exec the file and call
`main(["blender", "--"])` with the same flags.

A build refuses — and writes nothing — unless the footprints match the A-07 schedule, the wall
centreline reads 200 m and the docks 584 m off the vertices, no two materials share a visible plane,
every visible water surface stands at least 5 cm above the levelled ground under it (a sphere, not
the city's plane), and a grid rasterised from the collision reaches every door, counter, gate, pad
and ramp while reaching neither the lagoon nor the canal.

## What is in the FBX

24 meshes, all with their pivot at the city's origin: the levelled ground at the city centre. The
walking surface is `floor_m` (0.30 m) above it. Each mesh carries several material slots named
`MAT_Borlash_*`; each slot's colour, metalness, roughness and emission are in the manifest, linear,
because the importer does not carry them. Collision is `UCX_<mesh>_NNN` convex hulls, one per solid,
only where a person or a ship can reach — no roofs, domes or spires. `SM_Borlash_Scenery` is the 41
closed buildings that fill the districts: one hull each, no doors (Joe, 2 October: scenery for now).

Blender's +Y is north. The FBX importer turns Blender `(x, y, z)` metres into Unreal `(x, -y, z)`
centimetres ×100, so north is **-Y** in Unreal. Every position in the manifest is in Blender metres.

## The manifest

- `berths` — the four landing pads: where a ship docks (task 169), with the `short_name` the flight
  readout uses ("Borlash NE dock").
- `service_points` — 37 places a player stands to use a building, each with its service and facing.
  Some are marked `available: false` with the reason (task 171).
- `doors`, `gates`, `buildings` — what the route check reached, and each building's schedule row.
- `airspace` — the no-fly radius and ceiling, and the platform a ship may not be stepped out of.
- `pad_required` — the terrain the city needs levelled under it (task 168).
- `water` — each visible water surface's height, and how far it clears the levelled ground where it
  comes nearest the centre. `SpaceMMO.Terrain.CityWaterStandsAboveTheGround` checks it against the
  game's terrain.
- `scenery` — the closed buildings: footprint, height and roof.
- `checks` — what the build measured, including the FBX round trip.
- `deviations` — every place the model departs from sheet A-07, and why.
